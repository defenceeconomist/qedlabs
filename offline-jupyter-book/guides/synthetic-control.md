# Synthetic control guide

## Use this section when

An intervention affects one or a few aggregate units, a credible set of untreated
donors exists, and a sufficiently long pre-treatment outcome history can be used
to construct and audit a synthetic comparison.

## What this adds to Module 6

The section turns donor-pool intuition into explicit design work: inspect predictor
and outcome fit, read unit and predictor weights, calculate gaps, compare placebo
units, test donor exclusions, and consider augmentation only after establishing a
classical benchmark.

## Recommended sequence

1. **Methods report:** define the treated unit, intervention, donor logic, and
   comparative estimand.
2. **Mechanics:** inspect the objects, weights, paths, and gaps behind a classical
   synthetic control.
3. **Proposition 99:** complete a canonical applied workflow with unit placebos.
4. **Augmentation:** compare classical and ridge-augmented specifications.
5. **Design and donor-pool worksheets:** document the design choices for a new case.

## Completion check

You should be able to justify every donor inclusion and exclusion, diagnose
pre-treatment fit before interpreting effects, distinguish a large post-treatment
gap from strong comparative evidence, and explain how placebos and sensitivity
checks qualify the conclusion.
