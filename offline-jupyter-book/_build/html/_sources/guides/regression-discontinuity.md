# Regression discontinuity guide

## Use this section when

Treatment eligibility or take-up changes at a known cutoff in a continuously
measured assignment variable, and observations sufficiently close to that cutoff
can support a local comparison.

## What this adds to Module 6

The section moves from recognising a threshold design to defining sharp and fuzzy
local effects, estimating at a boundary, distinguishing conventional from
bias-corrected inference, and interrogating continuity with density, covariate,
bandwidth, placebo, and donut checks.

## Recommended sequence

1. **Methods report:** connect the institutional assignment rule to the estimand.
2. **Sharp RD foundations:** plot and calculate a transparent local jump.
3. **Design diagnostics:** examine manipulation, covariates, bandwidths, and
   false cutoffs.
4. **Fuzzy RD extension:** separate the first stage, reduced form, and local IV
   estimand when the cutoff changes treatment probability rather than treatment
   deterministically.

## Completion check

Before leaving the section, you should be able to state why the effect is local,
defend the score orientation and cutoff, explain the chosen bandwidth, distinguish
diagnostic evidence from proof, and report both the treatment discontinuity and
outcome discontinuity in a fuzzy design.
